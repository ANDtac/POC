## Oak Challenge Tracker

The guide data in `backend/data/guides/` is converted from the Oak Challenge Tracker
(https://github.com/EzraSkwarka/OakChallenge) by `backend/scripts/import-oak-tracker.js`.
The source commit is recorded in each guide file under `source.data.commit`.
That project bases its progression on the Professor Oak Challenge guides by u/mewlax84
(https://www.reddit.com/r/ProfessorOak/comments/bj6yeh/professor_oak_challenge_guides/).
